PRIVACY POLICY

[Last updated: October 2026]

1. Who is responsible

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

SynBio Team of the University of Stuttgart

Allmandring 31

Further details can be found in our Legal Notice.

2. Overview

We keep the processing of personal data to a minimum. This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, advertising, or embedded content from third parties (such as videos, maps or social media plugins).

3. Hosting and server log files

When you open this website, the server that hosts it automatically records information that your browser transmits. This includes:

  • your IP address,
  • the date and time of the request,
  • the page or file requested,
  • the website from which you came (referrer),
  • the browser and operating system you use.

This data is needed to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable. It is not combined with other data sources. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the technically error-free and secure operation of the website. Log files are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed for this purpose.

4. Fonts

The fonts used on this website are stored on our own server. When you visit the website, no connection is made to servers of Google or other font providers.

5. Local storage in your browser

If you close the “Work in Progress” notice or change the colour theme, the website saves this choice in the local storage of your browser so that it is remembered on your next visit. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Storing it is strictly necessary to provide the function you asked for (§ 25(2) TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

6. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process the data you send us (for example your name, email address and message) only to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or to joining or sponsoring the team, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise; our legitimate interest is responding to the enquiries sent to us. We delete your data once your request has been fully dealt with, unless legal retention periods require us to keep it.

7. Links to other websites

This website contains links to other websites, for example Instagram, LinkedIn, GitLab, iGEM and the University of Stuttgart. No data is transferred to these providers until you click a link. After that, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17),
  • restrict the processing of your data (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21).

To exercise these rights, simply write to us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg (LfDI Baden-Württemberg).

9. Changes to this policy

We update this privacy policy when the website or the legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
